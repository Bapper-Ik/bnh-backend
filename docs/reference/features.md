### Custodian — First-release features

1. **Authentication and account security** — Staff invitations, login, password recovery, profiles, and multifactor authentication.
2. **Staff and permissions management** — Manage staff accounts, roles, access rights, and account status.
3. **Organisation management** — Manage companies, departments, and assigned HODs and executive officeholders.
4. **Dashboard** — View request counts, recent activity, and outstanding tasks according to the user’s permissions.
5. **Vendor management** — Store vendor information, contacts, and bank details.
6. **Requisition management** — Create, save, edit, and submit requests with scope, cost items, and automatically calculated totals.
7. **Automatic approval routing** — Apply our agreed amount thresholds and requester-escalation rules, with no self-approval.
8. **Approval inbox** — Review assigned requests and approve, reject, or return them for correction.
9. **Board approval workflow** — The Company Secretary records the meeting resolution; a different person, the Board Chairman, reviews and signs off.
10. **Declarations and signature capture** — Capture the requester’s declaration and signatures tied to the exact request or decision.
11. **Document attachments** — Securely upload, view, and download supporting documents and Board-resolution evidence.
12. **Request tracking and revision history** — See the current status, required next action, previous versions, and decisions.
13. **Notifications** — In-app and email alerts for assigned tasks, decisions, and returned requests.
14. **PDF generation and export** — Generate requisition documents containing the request details and approval evidence.
15. **Immutable audit trail** — Preserve a searchable record of actions that application users cannot edit or delete.

**All of these belong to one integrated system. Payment processing and the legacy approval stages are excluded.**
